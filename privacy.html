Privacy Policy

Il Bisonte NYC Operations Hub · Last updated September 7, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Il Bisonte NYC Operations Hub handles information when authorized users access and use the private operations workspace hosted at operations.ilbisonte.propertydex.xyz.

1. Information we process

When you sign in with Google, the application may receive basic identity information associated with your Google account, including:

  • name;
  • email address;
  • Google account identifier;
  • profile image, when provided by Google;
  • sign-in and access-status information.

The workspace may also process operational information entered by authorized users, such as project records, requests, task information, vendor coordination notes, procedures, activity records, role assignments, notifications and project-management worklog entries.

2. How we use information

Information is used to authenticate users, manage access approval and roles, operate the workspace, maintain operational records, support project coordination, create audit/activity records, and send access or operational notifications where configured.

3. Google data

The application uses Google Sign-In for authentication. It is not designed to request access to Gmail message content, Google Drive file content, Google Calendar content, contacts, or other Google Workspace data through the browser login flow. Authentication data is used to verify identity and enforce the workspace’s own approval and role rules.

4. Storage and service providers

Operational application data is handled through the Google Apps Script backend and the Google Sheet configured for this workspace. The public web interface is hosted on the propertydex.xyz domain. Google may process authentication information as part of Google Sign-In according to Google’s own terms and privacy practices.

5. Passwords and secrets

The Operations Hub is not intended to store raw passwords, authentication secrets, private keys, or similar credentials. Where operational records reference credentials, the application should record only appropriate metadata such as ownership or approved credential location.

6. Access and sharing

Access to the private workspace is role-based and subject to approval. Information may be visible to authorized users according to their assigned role and operational permissions. We do not sell personal information.

7. Retention

Information may be retained for as long as needed for operational continuity, project records, access governance, audit history, or legitimate administrative purposes. Authorized administrators may update, restrict, or remove access records when appropriate.

8. Security

Reasonable safeguards are used, including Google authentication, backend identity verification, approval status, role-based permissions, and server-side authorization checks. No system can guarantee absolute security.

9. Your choices and requests

For questions about your account information, access status, correction, or removal requests relating to this workspace, contact peter@propertydex.xyz.

10. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as the workspace, hosting, ownership, or operational processes change. The latest version will be posted at this URL with an updated effective date.
