Il Bisonte NYC Operations Hub

A private operations and project-management workspace configured for authorized Il Bisonte New York operational use.

What the application does

The Operations Hub centralizes store requests, project coordination, vendor follow-up, procedures, operational records, role-based access and project-management work tracking in one workspace.

Access is restricted through Google Sign-In and an internal approval process. A Google account alone does not automatically grant access; each user must be approved and assigned an authorized role.

Authentication and access

The application uses Google Sign-In to verify a user’s identity. The Operations Hub does not ask users to create or store a separate application password. Authorization is managed separately through the workspace’s user and role records.

Data handling

Operational records are handled through the configured Google Apps Script backend and Google Sheet for this workspace. The application is not designed to store raw account passwords or secrets; credential ownership or location may be referenced without storing the underlying password.
